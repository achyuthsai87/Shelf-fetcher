    Store map

    Add your store plan

    Take a photo of your drawing or printout, or choose a PDF of it. It becomes a guide you trace over. The app then draws a clean 3D version of your store and makes the bottle glow where it sits.

    Trace your sections

    Swipe to scroll the page. Tap + Add a box, or tap a box and choose Add next one to get the next number beside it (A3 gives A4). You can also press and hold on the photo until it buzzes, then drag to draw a box. Tap a box to rename or delete it. Press and hold a box, then drag, to move it. After you tap a box it shows four corner squares to drag for resizing and a round handle on top to turn it. Edges snap to neighbouring boxes (switch that off below). Duplicate copies a box with its shelves. Tidy up straightens everything into a neat layout.

    Change many boxes at once

    Type the box names or ranges, pick a type, and tap Apply. For example 1-17, FD or C10-C17.

    Settings

    Add many at once

    Pick a CSV file or paste a list. Column headers like Brand, Flavor, Type, Size, Aisle, Shelf are matched by name, in any order. Bottles already listed get their location updated instead of duplicated.

    Undo stays available until you close the app. After that, use Automatic backups below: the newest one named “Before importing” is your list as it was.

    Back up or move to another phone

    Save every bottle as a CSV file. On the other phone, open Import a list and choose that file.

    App update

    Tap to fetch the newest version. Your bottles and map are kept.

    Back up everything

    Saves your bottles, the map and every section into one file. Tap Save backup, then choose Save to Files and pick your Google Drive folder. The phone remembers that folder, so next time it is the same two taps. To get everything back, tap Restore and pick the file.

    Merge adds bottles and newer edits from another phone's backup file. It deletes nothing and keeps this phone's map.

    Automatic backups

    Made before anything risky, like reloading the layout, replacing the map or importing a list, and about once a day. The newest 6 are kept on this phone.

    Stock alerts

    A bottle with a quantity at or below this number shows as Low. Zero or less shows as Out.

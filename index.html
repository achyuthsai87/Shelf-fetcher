    Store map

    Add your store plan

    Take a photo of your drawing or printout, even a rough one. It becomes a guide you trace over. The app then draws a clean 3D version of your store and makes the bottle glow where it sits.

    Trace your sections

    Drag on the photo to draw a box over each aisle or section. Tap a box to rename it, drag it to move it, and drag its corner square to resize it. Tidy up then straightens everything into a neat layout.

    Settings

    Add many at once

    Pick a CSV file or paste a list. Column headers like Brand, Flavor, Type, Size, Aisle, Shelf are matched by name, in any order. Bottles already listed get their location updated instead of duplicated.

    Back up or move to another phone

    Save every bottle as a CSV file. On the other phone, open Import a list and choose that file.

    App update

    Tap to fetch the newest version. Your bottles and map are kept.

    Back up everything

    Saves your bottles, the map and every section into one file. Tap Save backup, then pick Google Drive (or Files, Mail) in the share sheet. To get it all back, tap Restore and choose that file from Drive.

    Automatic backups

    Made before anything risky, like reloading the layout, replacing the map or importing a list, and about once a day. The newest 6 are kept on this phone.

    Where your list lives

    Everything is saved on this phone only and works with no signal. Clearing the browser's website data removes it, so export a copy now and then.
